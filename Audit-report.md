# Internal Security Audit Report – Botium Toys

## Introduction
This report presents the findings of an internal security audit conducted on Botium Toys. The audit evaluates the organization’s current security posture using the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF).

## Scope
The audit includes:
- Physical location (storefront and warehouse)
- Internal IT systems
- Online store infrastructure
- Customer and payment data

## Goals
- Identify security risks and vulnerabilities
- Evaluate existing controls
- Assess compliance with regulatory requirements
- Recommend improvements

## Summary of Findings
The audit revealed several critical security gaps:
- Lack of multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- No evidence of data encryption
- Absence of monitoring and detection systems
- No formal incident response plan

## Risk Level
**Overall Risk Level: HIGH**

The organization is at high risk due to weak security controls and lack of compliance with industry standards.
