# Risk Assessment – Botium Toys

## Risk 1: Weak Access Controls
**Description:** The organization does not enforce strong authentication mechanisms such as multi-factor authentication (MFA).  
**Impact:** Unauthorized access to systems and sensitive data.  
**Likelihood:** High  

---

## Risk 2: Unencrypted Sensitive Data
**Description:** There is no indication that customer or payment data is encrypted.  
**Impact:** Exposure of sensitive data, leading to financial loss and legal penalties.  
**Likelihood:** High  

---

## Risk 3: Lack of Monitoring Systems
**Description:** No monitoring or logging systems are in place to detect suspicious activity.  
**Impact:** Security incidents may go undetected.  
**Likelihood:** High  

---

## Risk 4: Compliance Violations
**Description:** The company operates internationally but does not meet GDPR or PCI DSS requirements.  
**Impact:** Regulatory fines and reputational damage.  
**Likelihood:** High
